## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added
- Merge PDF, with drag-and-drop ordering.
- Split PDF by ranges, every N pages or one page per file, with ZIP export.
- Extract pages and delete pages, with click, Shift+click or typed selection.
- Reorder pages: drag and drop, rotate, duplicate, delete, append pages from other PDFs.
- Rotate pages, on top of the existing rotation.
- Sign PDF with a drawn or imported signature, movable and resizable.
- Images to PDF (JPG/PNG) with page size, orientation, margins and EXIF orientation fix.
- Compress PDF, lossless or lossy in three levels.
- Text watermark, centered or tiled, with live preview.
- English and French interface, detected from the browser and switchable.
- Light and dark themes.
- Automatic deployment to GitHub Pages on every push to `main`.
